Our Story

Environmental
Expertise Since 1973

Established in 1973, ENTECH Environmental Consultants Ltd. is one of British Columbia’s longest-established environmental consulting firms, bringing over 50 years of experience in environmental science, site investigations, and remediation.

From our early involvement in environmental impact assessments, ecological studies, and natural resource management, ENTECH has evolved to provide a comprehensive range of environmental consulting services for residential, commercial, and industrial properties throughout British Columbia.

Our team combines decades of environmental consulting experience with practical, hands-on expertise to deliver reliable, cost-effective solutions. We work closely with property owners, developers, contractors, and regulatory authorities to help clients navigate environmental challenges, meet regulatory requirements, and move their projects forward.

Our Services

Practical Environmental Solutions

ENTECH provides a range of environmental consulting services, from preliminary site assessments to detailed investigations, contaminated site remediation, and regulatory reporting.

  • Environmental Site Assessments

    Phase I and Phase II Environmental Site Assessments to identify potential environmental concerns, investigate site conditions, and support property transactions and redevelopment.

  • Contaminated Site Investigation & Remediation
