Government & Environmental
Regulations

  • BC Ministry of Environment and Parks Provincial environmental policies, programs, and regulatory information. env.gov.bc.ca
  • BC Contaminated Sites Regulation (CSR) Environmental standards and requirements for contaminated site investigation and remediation. bclaws.gov.bc.ca
  • BC Site Remediation Program Guidance on contaminated site investigation, remediation, and environmental reporting. www2.gov.bc.ca
02/04

Environmental Permitting &
Natural Resources

  • FrontCounter BC — Natural Resource Permitting Provincial applications and authorizations for land, water, forestry, mining, and resource development. www2.gov.bc.ca
  • BC Environmental Assessment Office Environmental assessment requirements and processes for major development projects. www2.gov.bc.ca
  • Fisheries and Oceans Canada (DFO) Federal guidance concerning fisheries, aquatic ecosystems, and fish habitat protection. dfo-mpo.gc.ca
  • BC Ministry of Forests Forestry management, resource development, and forest-related regulations. www2.gov.bc.ca
  • BC Ministry of Mining and Critical Minerals Mining regulations, mineral exploration, and mine permitting information. www2.gov.bc.ca
