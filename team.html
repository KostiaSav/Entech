Founder

S. Fredrik (Fred) Sverre

M.Sc., R.P.Bio.

Founder, President & Principal Environmental Consultant

S. Fredrik (Fred) Sverre is a pioneer in environmental consulting and the founder of ENTECH Environmental Consultants Ltd., established in 1973. With more than five decades of professional experience, Fred has played a significant role in advancing environmental assessment, ecological research, natural resource management, and environmental consulting in British Columbia and beyond.

Fred is a founding member of British Columbia’s College of Applied Biologists and holds the distinguished registration number R.P.Bio. #2, reflecting his early involvement in establishing professional biology in the province.

Throughout his career, Fred has directed and contributed to numerous environmental investigations for federal and provincial government agencies, industrial organizations, resource development companies, and private sector clients, including work associated with Vancouver International Airport, the Kootenay River Diversion Project, the Columbia Wetlands, fisheries and aquaculture development in Clayoquot Sound, and mining-related environmental assessments.

As ENTECH’s founder and principal consultant, Fred continues to provide senior technical direction and project oversight, combining decades of scientific expertise with a practical approach to complex environmental challenges.

Paul Scott

M.Sc., P.Geo.

Senior Hydrogeologist

  • Hydrogeology
  • Groundwater monitoring
  • Contaminant assessment

Paul Scott is a registered Professional Geologist (P.Geo.) with over 30 years of international experience in environmental consulting and hydrogeology. His expertise includes contaminated site investigations, groundwater remediation, hydrogeological assessments in tidally influenced environments, environmental data modelling, and expert witness testimony.

Throughout his career, Paul has worked on a wide range of industrial, commercial, and energy-related projects, including oil refineries, petrochemical facilities, service stations, landfills, power stations, rail yards, and military installations. His international experience spans Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States, France, Oman, and Saudi Arabia.

Paul has served as a Senior Hydrogeologist with ENTECH Environmental Consultants Ltd. since 2008, providing technical expertise in hydrogeological investigations, remediation design, and environmental project management. Previously, he held consulting positions with WorleyParsons, Hemmera, Golder Associates, Stantec, and URS Corporation.
